      Power Tariff Subsidy & Electricity Duty Exemption in Gujarat (2026)

      22 NOV 2026 Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026

      Tackling Industrial Electricity Costs in Gujarat: The 5-Year Relief Package

      In high-output manufacturing and service enterprises, electric power is rarely a minor overhead; it is often the single highest recurring operational expense after raw materials. Between industrial HT/LT tariffs, fuel surcharges (FPPPA), and state electricity duty, Gujarat enterprises frequently pay between ₹7.50 to ₹9.20 per kilowatt-hour (kWh).

      To safeguard the competitive edge of Gujarat's industrial ecosystem, the Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 deploys a potent two-fold energy relief mechanism: Power Tariff Subsidy (direct tariff relief of ₹1.00 to ₹2.00 per unit) combined with a statutory 100% Electricity Duty Exemption for 5 continuous years.

      Pillar 1: Power Tariff Relief

      • Direct concession of ₹1.00 to ₹2.00 per unit (kWh) on billed consumption.
      • Applicable for 5 consecutive years from Date of Commercial Production (DoCP).
      • Reimbursed through DIC Gujarat directly or credited against energy bills.

      Pillar 2: Electricity Duty Exemption

      • 100% complete waiver of state electricity duty for 5 years.
      • Granted under the Gujarat Electricity Duty Act by the Chief Electrical Inspectorate (CEI).
      • Instantly wipes out 15% to 20% of your total billed amount right at generation!

      1. Sector-Wise Breakdown: Who Qualifies for Power Incentives?

      Gujarat's power incentives are engineered to support energy-demanding enterprises across diverse industrial verticals:

      Heavy Manufacturing & Foundry

      Induction furnaces, steel re-rolling, aluminum extrusions, injection moulding, and chemical reaction plants operating 24x7 in Sanand, Hazira, and Dahej recover tremendous monthly margins.

      Textile Weaving & Dyeing Mills

      Textile processing, yarn spinning, and modern jet looms in Surat and Ahmedabad are exceptionally power-hungry. Power concessions keep Gujarat textile products price-competitive worldwide.

      Cold Storages & Agro-Processing

      Cold chain infrastructure for horticulture, dairy processing, and seafood exporters require continuous chillers. Special tariff reductions prevent post-harvest spoilage and lower preservation costs.

      Hotels, Resorts & Tourism Units

      Eligible tourism units, resort properties, and hotels outside municipal corporation limits qualify for electricity duty exemptions, significantly offsetting central HVAC and lighting overheads.

      Hospitals & Healthcare Centers

      Healthcare units running ICU lifelines, medical imaging, and clean-air ventilation round-the-clock can avail of state electricity duty exemptions to lower recurring operational strain.

      IT Parks & Data Centers

      Hyperscale data centers, server server farms, and ESDM assembly plants benefit from specialized power tariffs and green open-access power integration in Gujarat.

      3. How to Apply & Claim Your Power Relief

      Securing full electricity benefits requires two synchronized filings:

      1. Chief Electrical Inspector (CEI) Filing: Immediately after obtaining the industrial power release and installation approval from CEI Gandhinagar, file the formal application for Electricity Duty Exemption under Section 3(2) of the Gujarat Electricity Duty Act.
      2. Submission to DISCOM: Submit the CEI sanction letter to your local DISCOM operational division (UGVCL, PGVCL, DGVCL, MGVCL, or Torrent). Once registered, the electricity duty component is zeroed out on subsequent monthly invoices.
      3. DIC Power Tariff Subsidy Application: File the online claim on the Gujarat Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP) submitting certified copies of paid electricity bills, meter readings, and proof of commercial manufacturing.
      4. Subsidy Disbursement: DIC approves and releases the per-unit subsidy reimbursement directly into your corporate account on a periodic basis.
      Crucial Pro-Tip: File Before Commercial Billing Starts!

      Retrospective claims for electricity duty exemption can take substantial administrative effort to adjust. Always initiate your CEI exemption file during the electrical installation phase so that duty waiver kicks in from your very first commercial bill.
