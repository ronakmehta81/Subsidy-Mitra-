Understanding the ATMANIRBHAR BHARAT Schemes

The "Aatmanirbhar Bharat" (Self-Reliant India) initiative has reshaped the economic landscape of India, particularly for MSMEs. By reducing import dependency and strengthening the local supply chain, the government has unleashed a wave of schemes, grants, and collateral-free loans. Here is a breakdown of what this means for your business.

1. Redefined MSME Criteria

One of the foundational steps of this initiative was redefining MSMEs to include both investment and annual turnover. For instance, a medium enterprise can now have up to ₹50 Crore in investment and ₹250 Crore in turnover. This allows growing businesses to scale without losing their valuable MSME benefits.

2. Collateral-Free Automatic Loans (CGTMSE)

Access to working capital has always been a pain point. The scheme infused massive liquidity by offering collateral-free automatic loans to businesses, fully guaranteed by the government. This allows MSMEs to meet operational liabilities, buy raw materials, and restart businesses without pledging their assets.

3. Equity Infusion through MSME Fund of Funds

For MSMEs with high growth potential, the government introduced a Fund of Funds to provide equity funding. This helps MSMEs scale up operations, expand their capacity, and even list on stock exchanges in the future.

4. Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme

While PLI initially targeted large scale industries, its trickle-down effect deeply benefits MSMEs acting as ancillaries and component suppliers. Furthermore, specific PLI schemes have been introduced to incentivize local manufacturing across sectors like electronics, pharmaceuticals, and textiles.

5. Disallowing Global Tenders

To protect Indian businesses from unfair global competition, global tenders have been disallowed in government procurement tenders up to ₹200 Crore. This guarantees that local MSMEs have a massive captive market for government contracts.
