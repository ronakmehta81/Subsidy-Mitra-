      Interest Subsidy in Gujarat 2026: 7% Term Loan Subvention Guide

      22 NOV 2026 Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026

      How Gujarat's 7% Interest Subsidy Slashes Your Real Borrowing Cost

      When financing a new factory or expanding production capacity, bank term loans are indispensable. However, commercial borrowing rates typically hover between 8.5% and 10.5% per annum. Over a 5-to-7-year loan tenure, cumulative interest payments can swallow a massive portion of operating margins.

      To solve this, the Government of Gujarat instituted the Interest Subvention Scheme under the Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026. Under this scheme, eligible enterprises receive an interest reimbursement of up to 7% per annum on term loans sanctioned for plant, machinery, and factory sheds. This reduces your effective borrowing rate down to an ultra-competitive 1.5% to 3%!

      Real-World Math: How 7% Interest Subsidy Works

      Bank Term Loan Amount:

      ₹1,00,00,000 (₹1 Crore)

      Bank Sanctioned Interest Rate:

      9.50% p.a.

      Gujarat Government Subvention:

      - 7.00% p.a.

      Net Effective Interest Borne by You:

      Only 2.50% p.a.!

      Result: On a ₹1 Crore machinery loan repaid over 5 years, this single subsidy reimburses approximately ₹20 Lakhs to ₹28 Lakhs directly back to your business, radically shortening your project payback period.

      2. Sector-by-Sector Eligibility: Who Can Benefit?

      The 7% Interest Subsidy under Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy covers multiple prominent industry segments across Gujarat:

      Manufacturing & Heavy Engineering

      Machinery loans for CNC turning, sheet metal forging, chemical reactors, plastics, and pharmaceutical formulation plants qualify for interest subvention on term loans taken for fixed capital assets.

      Textile, Weaving & Processing

      Spinning mills, air-jet loom weaving units, technical textile installations, and garmenting factories in Surat and Ahmedabad can combine state interest subvention with central credit support schemes.

      Hotels, Resorts & Hospitality

      Tourism and hospitality projects (hotels, tent cities, adventure retreats, and theme parks) eligible under Gujarat Tourism Policy can claim term loan interest subvention on civil construction and equipment loans.

      Hospitals & Diagnostic Healthcare

      New private multispecialty hospitals, pathology labs, and advanced scan centers (128-slice CT, 3T MRI) taking high-value term loans for advanced healthcare gear qualify for regular interest subvention.

      IT & Software Technology Parks

      Enterprise server rooms, AI compute clusters, cloud datacenters, and ESDM hardware development hubs obtain interest reimbursement on high-performance IT infrastructure term loans.

      3. Eligible Lending Institutions

      To qualify for Gujarat Government interest subsidy, the term loan must be disbursed by a recognized formal banking channel:

      • All Public Sector Scheduled Commercial Banks (State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank, Union Bank, etc.)
      • All Private Sector Scheduled Commercial Banks (HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IndusInd Bank, etc.)
      • State Financial Corporations: Gujarat State Financial Corporation (GSFC) and Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
      • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) approved Urban Cooperative Banks and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

      4. The Quarterly DIC Claim Reimbursement Workflow

      Unlike capital subsidy which is disbursed in tranches, interest subsidy is reimbursed quarterly or semi-annually upon submitting actual proof of interest paid to your lender:

      1. In-Principle Registration: Apply on the Gujarat Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP) prior to commercial production or within the prescribed policy window.
      2. Quarterly Bank Certificate: At the close of each quarter, collect an authorized interest calculation certificate and loan statement from your lending bank branch.
      3. Chartered Accountant (CA) Attestation: Have your CA certify Form-2 indicating exact interest debited and paid on eligible fixed assets (excluding working capital interest).
      4. Online Claim Upload: Submit the quarterly claim on the IFP portal within the stipulated time limit.
      5. Direct Bank Credit: Upon DIC officer verification, subsidy funds are directly credited into your term loan account, offsetting subsequent installments.
      Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Disqualification Rule

      If your loan account defaults or is categorized as SMA-2/NPA during any quarter, the interest subsidy for that quarter is forfeited. Timely debt servicing is a non-negotiable prerequisite under DIC Gujarat norms.
