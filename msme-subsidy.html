Essential Documents Checklist

Having these essential documents ready ensures your subsidy application moves quickly without delays or unwanted department queries.

Statutory & Business Registrations

  • Udyam Registration: Active certificate with eligible NIC codes.
  • Entity Constitution: Partnership Deed, COI, MOA/AOA, or LLP.
  • PAN & GSTIN: Company PAN card copy & active GST certificate.
  • Premises Ownership: Land Sale Deed, 7/12, or Registered Lease.
  • Pollution Consent: Valid GPCB CTE or CCA approval letter.

Financial & Technical Documentation

  • Bank Sanction Letter: Official term loan sanction & appraisal.
  • Machinery Invoices: Vendor quotes & electronic bank payment slips.
  • Engineer Certificate: Chartered Engineer valuation for machinery.
  • CA Certificate: Chartered Accountant gross fixed asset statement.
  • Audited Financials: Last 3 years Balance Sheets, P&L & ITR files.

Policy FAQ

Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 FAQs

Everything you need to know about eligibility, Taluka categories (A & B), capital and interest subsidies, power relief, and application deadlines.

Under the Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026, eligible enterprises can secure three primary financial incentives:
  • Capital Subsidy: Up to 35% on eligible plant and machinery investments.
  • Interest Subsidy: 7% per annum on term loans for 5 years, drastically lowering your loan EMI burden.
  • Power Tariff Subsidy: Up to ₹2 per unit for 5 years, reducing monthly factory electricity costs.

The Vikshit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 divides all talukas into two categories to promote balanced regional growth:
  • Taluka Category A (Underdeveloped Talukas): Eligible for highest support — 35% Capital Subsidy, 7% Interest Subsidy (5 years), and ₹2/unit Power Tariff Subsidy (5 years).
  • Taluka Category B (Developed Talukas): Eligible for 25% Capital Subsidy, 7% Interest Subsidy (5 years), and ₹1/unit Power Tariff Subsidy (5 years).
