Government Incentives

The 4 Pillars of Gujarat MSME Subsidies

Under the Gujarat Industrial Policy (Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme), manufacturing and eligible service enterprises qualify for direct capital grants and prolonged operational cost reductions.

Direct Financial Grant

Capital Investment Subsidy

Direct government grant reimbursed against eligible investment in plant, machinery, and equipment for establishing new setups or expanding existing units.

  • Micro Units: Up to 25% subsidy (Max ₹35 Lakhs).
  • Small Units: Up to 20% subsidy (Max ₹75 Lakhs).
  • Medium Units: Up to 10% subsidy (Max ₹1.25 Crore).
  • Special Bonus: Extra 1%–2% for Women, Youth & SC/ST.
Cash grant credited directly to your business loan or escrow account.
EMI Interest Reduction

Term Loan Interest Subvention

Substantial annual interest rate subsidy on institutional bank term loans, drastically lowering finance costs during the initial debt servicing years.

  • Category 1 Talukas: 7% interest subsidy for 7 years.
  • Category 2 Talukas: 6% interest subsidy for 6 years.
  • Category 3 Talukas: 5% interest subsidy for 5 years.
  • Annual Ceiling: Up to ₹35 Lakhs/year claimed half-yearly.
Effectively lowers your bank borrowing rate to just 3%–5% net interest.
Tax Cashflow Incentive

Net SGST Reimbursement

Reimbursement of State GST (SGST) paid on the sale of manufactured goods, helping manufacturing units retain substantial liquidity over a 10-year period.

  • 100% Reimbursement: On eligible Net SGST paid in Gujarat.
  • 10-Year Horizon: Claimable annually for 10 consecutive years.
  • Eligible Cap: Cumulative claim up to 100% of fixed capex.
  • Unit Expansion: Existing units with 25%+ fresh capex qualify.
Transforms indirect tax outflows into direct operational working capital.
Utility & Quality Grants

Power Concession & Technology Grants

Direct operational relief on electricity costs alongside financial assistance for ERP, technology adoption, quality standards, and intellectual property.

  • Power Concession: ₹1/unit electricity rebate for 5 years.
  • ERP & Software: Up to ₹10 Lakhs grant (65% assistance).
  • Quality & Standards: Up to 80% reimbursement for ISO/ZED.
  • Patents & R&D: Up to ₹25 Lakhs support for IP filings.
Ensures compliance, operational efficiency, and global export competitiveness.
Application Readiness

Essential Documents Checklist

Having these documents verified and compiled avoids application queries and guarantees swift in-principle sanction by the District Industries Centre (DIC).

Statutory & Business Registrations

  • Udyam Registration: Active certificate with eligible NIC codes.
  • Entity Constitution: Partnership Deed, COI, MOA/AOA, or LLP.
  • PAN & GSTIN: Company PAN card copy & active GST certificate.
  • Premises Ownership: Land Sale Deed, 7/12, or Registered Lease.
  • Statutory NOCs: Approved Factory Plan, NA Order & Fire NOC.
  • Pollution Consent: Valid GPCB CTE or CCA approval letter.

Financial & Technical Documentation

  • Bank Sanction Letter: Official term loan sanction & appraisal.
  • Project Report (DPR): Complete techno-commercial viability study.
  • Machinery Invoices: Vendor quotes & electronic bank payment slips.
  • Engineer Certificate: Chartered Engineer valuation for machinery.
  • CA Certificate: Chartered Accountant gross fixed asset statement.
  • Audited Financials: Last 3 years Balance Sheets, P&L & ITR files.
Critical Timing Rule (Gujarat Industrial Policy):

Provisional subsidy registration on the official Industries Commissionerate portal must be submitted before or within designated milestones prior to commercial production. The Gujarat government categorizes all talukas into Category 1, Category 2, and Category 3 based on economic and industrial development.
  • Category 1 (Developing/Less Developed Talukas): Highest incentives — 25% Capital Subsidy + 7% Interest Subsidy for 7 years.
  • Category 2 (Moderately Developed Talukas): 20% Capital Subsidy + 6% Interest Subsidy for 6 years.
  • Category 3 (Developed Talukas like Ahmedabad/Vadodara municipal limits): 10%–15% Capital Subsidy + 5% Interest Subsidy for 5 years.
